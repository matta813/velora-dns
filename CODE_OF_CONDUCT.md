# Code of Conduct

Velora DNS welcomes people of different backgrounds and experience levels. This
code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project
spaces, including when representing the project elsewhere.

Be considerate and constructive. Assume good intent, explain technical objections
clearly, and make room for others to participate. Disagreement about ideas is fine;
personal attacks are not.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption,
and publishing another person's private information without permission are not
acceptable. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, temporarily restrict
participation, or ban people, depending on the severity and pattern of behavior.

To report misconduct, contact the maintainer privately through
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/matta813). If no private contact method is
available, open an issue asking for a private reporting channel **without describing
the incident or identifying people publicly**. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical; the maintainer will review the context, avoid conflicts
of interest, and communicate the outcome to the reporter when possible.

This policy follows the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
